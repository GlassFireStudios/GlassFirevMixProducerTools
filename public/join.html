Welcome

You've been invited to join a live show. This takes about two minutes and checks that everything works before you go on.

  • Find a quiet spot with light on your face.
  • Plug in headphones if you have them. Wired is best.
  • Close other video apps (Zoom, Teams, FaceTime).

Checking your browser

Camera and microphone

Your browser will ask to use your camera and microphone. Press Allow.

Say something. The bar should move.

Can you hear us?

Press play. You should hear a short chime.

    The producer can see and hear you before you go on air, so there's no need to rush.

    Something not working?
    • No camera or mic prompt: click the camera icon in the address bar and choose Allow, then press "Ask again".
    • iPhone or iPad: use Safari. In Settings, go to Safari, then Camera and Microphone, and choose Allow.
    • Opened from Gmail, Facebook or Instagram? Open this link in Chrome or Safari instead.
    • Work laptop? Company firewalls can block video calls. Try your phone on cellular data.
    • Still stuck? Reply to the producer who sent this link. They can see how far you got.
